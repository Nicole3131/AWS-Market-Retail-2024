# Market Intelligence Agent: Retail Strategy 2024

## 📌 Project Overview
This project involves the development of a no-code Market Intelligence Agent built within Amazon QuickSuite. The goal of the agent is to support executive decision-making by synthesizing internal transaction data with external market signals to produce decision-grade intelligence. 

Instead of manually sifting through scattered research, this workflow automatically evaluates dataset trends, gathers relevant market context, and packages the findings into a leadership-ready intelligence brief.

## 💼 Business Scenario
A retail business leadership team needs to allocate their Q3/Q4 marketing budget efficiently. They require actionable insights regarding consumer purchasing dynamics across three main product categories: Electronics, Clothing, and Beauty. 

## ⚙️ Tools & Tech Stack
*   **Platform:** Amazon QuickSuite (Agents, Spaces)
*   **External Signal Gathering:** Quick Research
*   **Internal Knowledge Base:** Kaggle Retail Sales Dataset

## 🏗️ Intelligence Workflow
The project is structured around two major functions:

1.  **The Intelligence Builder:**
    *   Integrated a Kaggle dataset as the internal knowledge base.
    *   Configured Quick Research to gather external signals, such as 2024 consumer spending shifts and the impact of inflation.
2.  **The Executive Intelligence Pack:**
    *   **Market Analysis:** Synthesized internal metrics (Total Amount, Quantity) with external macroeconomic trends.
    *   **Reliability Evaluation:** Documented confidence levels, identified missing data contexts (e.g., net profitability), and established strict limitations to prevent overconfident AI hallucinations.
    *   **Market Intelligence Brief:** Delivered 3 strategic insights and an Impact vs. Effort matrix for actionable next steps.

## 📊 Key Strategic Insights
1.  **Electronics as the Core Revenue Driver:** Consumers aged 18-35 generate the highest gross revenue in the tech category, heavily supported by the adoption of Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) platforms.
2.  **Beauty as a Customer Acquisition Engine:** While generating lower individual order values, the Beauty category drives massive transaction volumes fueled by female demographics and social commerce trends.
3.  **Clothing Sector Ambiguity:** The apparel category lacks a distinct demographic identity, signaling a need for a marketing repositioning or cross-selling loyalty programs.
